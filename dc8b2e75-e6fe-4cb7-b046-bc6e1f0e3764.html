How You Make Decisions

When you honor that signal, your life organizes itself; when you override it with logic or politeness, everything gets heavier. The signal is fast and it's never wrong. Your work is simply to notice it, trust it, and act on it before the mind talks you out of it.

Lessons You'll Learn
Starting is easy for you; the harder art is waiting for the right thing to start.

Your fire wants to charge. But the deepest truth of how you're built is that you're meant to respond, not initiate from nothing. When you launch at something because you're bored or restless, it stalls. When you wait for something to show up and your body lights up to meet it, it flies. The recurring trap is forcing momentum when the signal hasn't come yet. Learn the difference between restless and ready. One burns you out. The other builds a life.

Lessons You'll Learn

What Alignment Feels Like
